Executive Summary
2025 IQAir World Air Quality Report · 20 Cities Analyzed
Analysis of 20 major global cities based on the IQAir 2025 World Air Quality Report reveals a stark divide in environmental health. Loni, India is the most polluted city (PM2.5: 112.5 µg/m³ — 22× WHO guideline), while Nieuwoudtville, South Africa is the world's cleanest (PM2.5: 1.0 µg/m³). Only 14% of global cities meet WHO PM2.5 guidelines (down from 17% in 2024). Average AQI across the analyzed set is 128. The most surprising observation: three Irish towns appeared among the most polluted in early 2026 rankings — a reminder that pollution is not confined to industrial regions. Central & South Asia hosts 17 of the world's 20 most polluted cities. Immediate action on indoor filtration and protective measures is recommended for high-AQI cities.

Personalised Recommendations
Actionable guidance based on your selected city's environmental data
🔵
HEPA Air Purifier: Use a HEPA H13+ certified purifier in bedroom and living room. Run 24/7 in high-AQI cities. Target indoor AQI below 25 regardless of outdoor levels.
🌿
Air-Purifying Plants: Peace lily, snake plant, and spider plant can reduce VOCs by up to 25%. Place 2–3 plants per room for measurable improvement.
🪟
Window Sealing: In cities with AQI >150, seal window gaps with weatherstripping. Outdoor air infiltration is a major source of indoor PM2.5.
🧹
Vacuum with HEPA Filter: Use a HEPA vacuum 3× per week to remove settled particulates. Avoid dry-sweeping which re-suspends PM10 particles.
💧
Humidifier (30–50% RH): Maintain indoor humidity between 30–50%. Low humidity allows fine particles to remain airborne longer and irritates respiratory mucosa.
⏰
Optimal Exercise Times: In polluted cities, outdoor exercise is best 6–8 AM (before traffic peaks) or after 8 PM. Avoid 3–7 PM when ozone and PM2.5 peak.
😷
N95/P100 Mask: Wear a properly fitted N95 mask for outdoor exercise in cities with AQI >100. Standard cloth masks filter less than 20% of PM2.5.
🌬️
Check AQI Before Going Out: Use IQAir app or AQI.in for real-time monitoring. Cancel outdoor exercise if AQI >150. Move workouts indoors on high-pollution days.
🌲
Choose Greener Routes: Parks and tree-lined streets can have AQI up to 30% lower than roadside areas. Plan walks through parks and avoid high-traffic corridors.
🚿
Shower Filter: Install a KDF/carbon shower filter to remove chlorine, heavy metals, and particulates from water. Hard water and chlorine are the primary causes of hair damage in urban cities.
🧴
Sulphate-Free Shampoo: Use sulphate-free, pH-balanced (4.5–5.5) shampoo. Sulphates strip natural oils from hair already stressed by pollutant particles clinging to the shaft.
💆
Scalp Detox Weekly: Use a gentle clarifying scalp scrub once a week to remove PM2.5 and PM10 that settles on the scalp and clogs hair follicles causing hair fall.
🥑
Deep Conditioning: Apply a deep conditioning mask (argan oil, shea butter) fortnightly. Pollution depletes hair lipids, causing brittleness and split ends.
🎩
Protective Covering: Wear a hat, scarf, or bun style when outdoors in high-pollution days. Physical barriers prevent particulate matter from depositing on the scalp and hair shaft.
🛡️
Antioxidant Serum (AM): Use Vitamin C + E serum every morning. These neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS) generated by PM2.5 particles that penetrate skin and accelerate aging.
☀️
SPF 50+ Daily: UV + pollution synergy amplifies skin damage. Use a broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen daily, even in winter months and on overcast days.
🧼
Double Cleanse at Night: First oil cleanser to dissolve pollution particles, then gentle foaming cleanser. PM2.5 adheres to sebum and must be removed to prevent pore-clogging and oxidative stress.
💧
Hyaluronic Acid + Ceramide Moisturizer: Pollution impairs skin barrier function. Repair it with HA for hydration and ceramides to strengthen the lipid barrier, especially in hard-water cities.
🍵
Niacinamide for Pollution Shield: 5–10% niacinamide serum strengthens the skin barrier, reduces inflammation, and minimizes pore size, reducing pollutant penetration by up to 30%.
🔵
RO + UV Water Filter: Install a Reverse Osmosis + UV purifier for drinking water in cities with poor water quality scores. Removes heavy metals, nitrates, bacteria, and microplastics.
🧪
Test Your Water: Get a home water quality test kit or lab test for TDS, hardness, chlorine, lead, and nitrates. Annual testing is recommended in cities near industrial zones.
💧
Drink 2.5–3L Daily: Increased water intake flushes airborne pollutants absorbed through the lungs and skin. Add electrolytes if exercising in high-AQI environments.
🚰
Whole-House Carbon Filter: For high-chlorine water cities, a whole-house GAC (granular activated carbon) filter protects skin, hair, and respiratory health in shower steam.
