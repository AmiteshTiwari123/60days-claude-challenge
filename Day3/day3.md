# Day 3 - Role Based Prompting

## Topic

Role-Based Prompting

## What I Learned

Role-Based Prompting means assigning a specific role or persona to an AI before asking a question.

Instead of receiving a generic response, the AI responds from the perspective of a specific expert.

Examples of roles:

* Developer
* Founder
* Product Manager
* HR Manager
* Marketer

## Why It Matters

Role-Based Prompting helps generate:

* More relevant answers
* Better quality outputs
* Expert-level insights
* More practical recommendations

## Experiment Performed

### Question

How can I build an AI education and workshop business as a B.Tech student in India?

### Without Role Prompt

Claude provided a general answer covering learning, business, and growth.

### Founder Persona

The response focused on:

* Business model
* Revenue generation
* Customer acquisition
* Growth strategy

### Developer Persona

The response focused on:

* Technical skills
* AI tools
* Project development
* Automation opportunities

## Key Learning

The same question can generate very different answers depending on the role assigned to the AI.

## Conclusion

Role-Based Prompting is a simple but powerful technique that improves the quality and usefulness of AI responses.
