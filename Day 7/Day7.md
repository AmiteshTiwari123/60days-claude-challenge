## Key Learnings

1. Different tasks require different models.
2. Sonnet provides the best balance of speed and quality.
3. High and Max effort should only be used for complex tasks.
4. Choosing the right model is as important as writing the right prompt.
5. AI productivity depends on both prompts and model selection.
