Loop Builder

Turn any one-shot prompt into an autonomous loop: the AI attempts the task, checks its own work against your criteria, and revises — until it meets your goal or hits a stop condition.

What's an "autonomous loop"? Normally you send one prompt and get one answer. A loop prompt instead tells the AI to evaluate its own answer and try again if it falls short — useful for tasks like "keep refining this code until all tests pass" or "keep revising this doc until it meets the checklist." The blocks below define the goal, how quality is judged, how the AI should improve each round, when to stop, and what it must never do while unsupervised.

What is this?

The short version

Personal AI Playbook is a local, single-file tool for building and reusing AI prompts, instead of rewriting them from scratch every time. Nothing here connects to the internet or an AI model directly — it helps you construct prompts, which you then paste into whichever AI you use.

Dashboard

A quick overview: how many workflows you have, your favorites, and your categories at a glance.

Workflows

Saved, reusable prompt templates grouped by task (debugging, docs, learning, etc). Each has fill-in variables — change a dropdown or a text field and the final prompt updates instantly, ready to copy.

Prompt Builder

Build a new prompt piece by piece from labeled blocks (role, objective, context, constraints, and more), each with a plain-language explanation of what it does. Good for one-off prompts or for designing a new reusable workflow.

Loop Builder

Takes a task and wraps it in instructions for the AI to self-evaluate and retry — an "autonomous loop." Useful when you want the AI to keep improving something against clear criteria rather than stopping after one attempt.
